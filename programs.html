Our Programs

Three programs designed to grow with your child — plus Seedlings and Summer Camps

Preschool Programs

All programs follow the Lake Central School District calendar (mid-August through mid-May). We are a very academic-based preschool with a Christian influence, focused on literacy development and academic, physical, social, and emotional growth.

🌱 Acorns Class

3-Year-Olds
Ages Must be 3 by October 1st and fully potty-trained
Schedule Mon/Wed 9:00–11:30 AM  or  Tue/Thu 9:30 AM–12:00 PM
Cost $182/month

Hands-on activities develop self-confidence, social/emotional skills, motor skills, and creativity. Introduces monthly themes, letters, numbers, Bible verses, and pre-reading/writing skills. A balanced mix of child-initiated play and teacher-directed lessons across literacy, math, science, social studies, fine arts, health, and technology.

🌳 Oaks Class

4-Year-Olds
Ages Must be 4 by October 1st
Schedule Mon/Tue/Thu 9:00–1:30 PM  or  Tue/Wed/Fri 9:30–2:00 PM
Half-day option (T/Th/F, dismissal 12:15): $225/month
Cost $317/month (full day)  |  $225/month (half day)
Bring Lunch and snack from home

Emphasizes academics — reading, writing, and math — to prepare students for kindergarten structure. Learning centers support teacher-directed activities and collaborative problem-solving, including language arts, construction, math, fine motor, art, dramatic play, science, sensory exploration, and iPad-based curriculum games.

🌲 Mighty Oaks Class

5-Year-Olds
Ages 5-year-olds or turning 5 by December 31st
Schedule Monday–Thursday, 9:00 AM–2:00 PM
Cost $410/month
Bring Lunch and snack from home

Designed for students with late kindergarten birthdays or those who would benefit from an additional year of preschool. Focuses on reading, proper writing technique, math, science applications, and sight words — modeled to align with the kindergarten curriculum.

🌿 Seedlings Class

Ages 2–4 with a Caregiver
Ages 2–4 years old, with a participating caregiver
Schedule Fridays, 9:15–10:00 AM
Sessions Session 1 (Sep 11 – Dec 11, 2026): $210
Session 2 (Jan 8 – Apr 30, 2027): $280

An interactive class featuring crafts, stories, learning centers, music, and play that introduces colors, numbers, and letters. A wonderful way for young children and their caregivers to explore learning together. Pre-registration required.

☀️ Summer Programs

Thematic Weekly Camps — Ages 3.5–6  |  June through July
Each week features a fun theme with activities, crafts, and exploration for young learners.

Kinder Bootcamp — June 29 – July 9  |  Mon–Thu, 9:00 AM–2:00 PM
Designed for kindergarten-bound students to build skills and confidence before their first day of school.
