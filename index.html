Welcome to White Oak

We offer three programs designed to grow with your child, staffed by credentialed educators who are passionate about early childhood learning.

About Us

Established in 2014, White Oak blends hands-on learning with individual attention in a safe, nurturing environment. We follow the Lake Central School District calendar and operate with a Christian influence.

Learn More

Our Programs

From our Acorns class for 3-year-olds to our Mighty Oaks class for pre-kindergartners, we have a program tailored to your child's stage of development. We also offer Seedlings and summer camps.

Meet Our Staff

Our highly-qualified teachers hold four-year degrees in education or a related field. Every staff member is CPR/first aid certified and brings a genuine love and passion for teaching young children.
